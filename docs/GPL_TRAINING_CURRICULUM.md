# GPL training curriculum

## Purpose

This curriculum turns GPL documentation into a practical learning path.

It is designed for a professional audience that needs to read, write and review GPL contracts without relying on the original author.

## Training promise

At the end of the curriculum, a learner should be able to:

- explain what GPL is and what it is not;
- read a GPL contract and describe its operational meaning;
- transform a human request into a GPL contract;
- apply the GPL validation checklist;
- understand how GPL can connect to GPV2, GL and GL_G.

## Level 1 - Read GPL

File: `docs/GPL_TRAINING_LEVEL_1_READ.md`

Goal:

```text
Understand the four GPL blocks and explain a GPL contract in plain language.
```

Learner outcome:

```text
Given a GPL contract, the learner can identify intention, emotion, glyphes and flux.
```

Estimated duration: 30 minutes.

## Level 2 - Write GPL

File: `docs/GPL_TRAINING_LEVEL_2_WRITE.md`

Goal:

```text
Convert a human request into a clean GPL contract.
```

Learner outcome:

```text
Given a human request, the learner can produce a first-pass GPL contract and review it with the checklist.
```

Estimated duration: 45 to 60 minutes.

## Level 3 - Compile GPL

File: `docs/GPL_TRAINING_LEVEL_3_COMPILE.md`

Goal:

```text
Understand how a GPL contract can continue toward GPV2, GL, GL_G and final rendering.
```

Learner outcome:

```text
Given a GPL contract, the learner can describe the downstream compilation path and preserve uncertainty.
```

Estimated duration: 60 to 90 minutes.

## Evaluation rubric

Use this rubric for simple assessment:

```text
PASS:
  The contract is readable, complete and preserves uncertainty.

REVISE:
  The contract is structurally present but vague, overloaded or weakly routed.

REJECT:
  The contract is missing required blocks, hides uncertainty or makes unsupported claims.
```

## Training boundary

This training teaches GPL as a documentation and contract layer. It does not certify runtime execution, legal compliance or factual truth by itself. Truth and validation require the surrounding governance layers.
